Share & export

Make a snapshot link to send round the group (everyone sees the current draw & standings), or download a results card for WhatsApp.

The snapshot link carries the data inside the URL itself — it's a frozen copy at the moment you copy it, not a live feed. For live, always-in-sync sharing you'd add the small backend (ask me).

Team tracker

Set how far each team has reached as the tournament goes, and knock teams out. A player is struck out once all their teams are gone. Goals (⚽) tally automatically from the scores you enter on Fixtures.

Match schedule

All 104 matches, kick-off times in UK time. Type the score in and it saves automatically — winners go green, goals feed the tables & leaderboard.

Group tables

Standings build automatically from the group-stage scores. Top two qualify; eight of the twelve third-placed teams also go through, so 3rd is "maybe".

Top 2 — through 3rd — maybe 4th — out

"Apply finished groups" sends the top two of every completed group through to the Round of 32 and knocks out the bottom team — you decide the third-placed ones by hand.

Live leaderboard

Each player's points come from all of their teams combined: +3 a group win, +1 a group draw, +1 per goal scored, plus a progress bonus for how far each team goes — R32 +4, R16 +8, QF +16, SF +28, Final +44, Champion +64. Knocked-out teams keep the points they earned.

The pot

Set the entry fee and split the pot across the prizes. Payouts show automatically once a winner is set.
